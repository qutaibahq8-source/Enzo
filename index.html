Our Story

The art of linen

Enzo was born from a love of linen — one of the oldest and most beautiful fabrics in the world. Spun from the flax plant, it is cool in the heat, warm in the cold, and grows softer with every wash.

Every piece is designed in small runs from carefully sourced European flax linen. We favour clean lines, natural undyed tones and the relaxed texture that makes linen feel lived-in from the very first wear.

— Enzo

Caring for Linen

Linen asks for very little — and rewards you with years of softness.

01

Wash Cool

Machine wash at 30–40°C on a gentle cycle with a mild detergent. Skip the bleach.

02

Air Dry

Hang or lay flat to dry in the shade. Linen dries quickly and keeps its shape best this way.

03

Embrace the Crease

A relaxed crease is part of linen’s charm. For a crisp finish, iron while slightly damp.

04

Store to Breathe

Fold loosely and keep in a cool, airy place — never sealed in plastic.

Good to Know

Will my linen shrink?

Our pieces are pre-washed, so shrinkage is minimal. Washing cool and air drying keeps the fit true over time.

How does the sizing run?

Most styles have a relaxed, easy fit. If you prefer a closer silhouette, we suggest sizing down. Each product notes its fit.

Is linen good for warm weather?

Yes — linen is highly breathable and draws moisture away from the skin, keeping you cool. It also layers beautifully in cooler months.

What is your returns policy?

Unworn pieces with their tags can be returned within 30 days for a full refund or exchange.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship worldwide. Shipping is complimentary on every order, and you will receive tracking as soon as your parcel leaves us.
