Our Story

Crafted with intention

Enzo was born from a simple belief: that clothing should feel as good as it looks, and last far longer than a season.

Every piece is designed in small runs, using carefully sourced wool, linen, cotton and silk. We favour clean lines, soft palettes and the quiet details that reveal themselves over time.

— Enzo
